Welcome to Let’s Connect

Living Word Baptist Church is a small, family-oriented Baptist church in Sweet Valley, PA, where people are welcomed, encouraged, and pointed to the truth of God’s Word. Whether you are new to church, looking for a church family, or simply searching for a place where faith is taught with conviction and care, LWBC is a place where you can grow, serve, and be part of a caring community of believers.

Pastor Raymond Purdy faithfully brings messages rooted in Scripture, with a heart to help people know Christ, follow Him, and live out their faith in everyday life. His preaching is what you will hear on the LWBC Let’s Connect Podcast, where Sunday’s messages are made available so you can listen again, catch up if you missed a service, or share biblical encouragement with someone else.

We would love to invite you to visit Living Word Baptist Church and experience the warmth of our church family for yourself. Come worship with us, meet Pastor Purdy and the people of LWBC, and see how God’s Word can make a difference in your life. Join us this Sunday — there is a place for you at Living Word Baptist Church.

Add to your home screen

Let’s Connect isn’t in the App Store — but your phone can install it straight from this site, with its own icon and no browser bar, in a few taps.

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) in Safari’s toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen.”
  3. Tap “Add” in the top right corner.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots) in Chrome’s toolbar — or tap “Install” directly if Chrome offers it.
  2. Tap “Add to Home screen” or “Install app.”
  3. Confirm by tapping “Add” or “Install.”

Once added, the icon opens the site full-screen, just like a downloaded app.
