Welcome to Let’s Connect

LWBC Let’s Connect is Pastor Raymond Purdy’s podcast — the preaching and teaching ministry of Living Word Baptist Church in Sweet Valley, Pennsylvania, carried past the church walls to wherever you happen to be listening. Each episode is a message rooted in Scripture and delivered plainly, the way Pastor Purdy delivers it from the pulpit on Sunday: no polish for polish’s sake, just the Word of God opened up and applied to real life.

Faith. Truth. Real talk. Real life. That’s the whole idea. Whether you’re a member of Living Word who missed a Sunday, someone in the Back Mountain looking for a church home, or a listener who found us from a long way off, you’re welcome here. Pick a door below and come on in.

Add to your home screen

Let’s Connect isn’t in the App Store — but your phone can install it straight from this site, with its own icon and no browser bar, in a few taps.

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) in Safari’s toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen.”
  3. Tap “Add” in the top right corner.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots) in Chrome’s toolbar — or tap “Install” directly if Chrome offers it.
  2. Tap “Add to Home screen” or “Install app.”
  3. Confirm by tapping “Add” or “Install.”

Once added, the icon opens the site full-screen, just like a downloaded app.
